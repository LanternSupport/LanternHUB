# 🏮 Lantern HUB - Roblox Islands Script

![Lantern HUB Showcase](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400.png?text=Lantern+HUB+Showcase+Image)

**Lantern HUB v1.0 (Beta)** is an advanced, highly optimized, and feature-rich script designed specifically for **Roblox Islands**. Built with the elegant Fluent UI design, it provides an all-in-one solution for farming, combat, market sniping, and world exploration.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🧑 Player Enhancements
- **WalkSpeed & JumpPower:** Modify your character's speed and jump height.
- **Noclip & Fly Mode:** Walk through walls or fly smoothly using camera navigation.
- **Freecam Mode:** Detach your camera to explore the island freely.

### 🌾 Farming Automation
- **Crop Automation:** Auto harvest and plant seeds. Auto plow grass into soil.
- **Timber Automation:** Auto chop trees within your radius or tween to distant trees.
- **Mining Automation:** Auto mine ores around you or tween to specific rocks.
- **Foraging Automation:** Smoothly navigate to and collect wild resources.

### ⚔️ Combat Automation
- **Kill Aura:** Automatically damage hostile mobs and bosses around you.
- **Auto Farm Mobs & Bosses:** Tween to enemies and auto-summon bosses when they die.
- **Auto Equip Weapon:** Never fight empty-handed; automatically re-equips your last weapon.

### 🛒 Market Sniper
- **Vending Sniper:** Automatically scans nearby vending machines and buys items instantly if they are below your target price.
- **Buy Anything Mode:** Snipes ANY item that falls below your set price limit.

### 📦 Utility & ESP (Misc)
- **Auto Fishing:** Fully automated casting and catching minigame.
- **World ESP:** See exactly what items and how many coins are inside Vending Machines and Chests through walls.
- **Island Utilities:** Join Code Spoofer, Auto Join Island, and Send Invites.
- **Inspect Inventory:** Peek into other players' inventories seamlessly.
- **Anti-AFK:** Bypass Roblox's idle disconnection (Error 268).
